What changes for your team

Less time chasing answers and repeating chores, more time on the work only people can do.

Engineering

Answers in seconds, not meetings.

Anyone can ask how a part of your product works and get a clear answer that shows exactly where it comes from. Small fixes arrive already tested and ready for a quick review, so engineers spend their time on what moves the business.

Releases

Ship faster without losing control.

Bothy prepares each release and waits. Nothing reaches your customers until the right people approve it, with two sign-offs where you want them, and everyone can see who said yes.

Your tools

Works inside the tools you already pay for.

Your team asks in Slack, Telegram, Discord or the web app. Bothy connects to GitHub, your ticketing, your docs and your internal systems, so work happens where it already lives. There is no new tool to learn.

Know-how

Knowledge stays with the company.

Bothy learns your processes, decisions and preferences as your team works with it. Anything shared with everyone is approved by an admin first, so what it knows stays accurate when people change roles or leave.

Routine work

The recurring busywork runs itself.

Daily stand-up summaries, morning briefings, invoice reminders, weekly progress reports. Set them up once and Bothy delivers them to the right channel, on time, every time.

Important decisions

Extra care for the answers that matter.

For pricing, planning and other high-stakes questions, Bothy works out several answers, checks them against each other and gives you the one they agree on, along with exactly what it cost.

Your data stays yours.

Built for companies that can't hand their code, customers or credentials to someone else's cloud. Every safeguard is on from day one.

Runs in your cloud
Install it on AWS or any server you control. Your data never leaves, and nothing is sent back to us.
Credentials locked away
Passwords and access keys are encrypted and only ever used with the service they belong to.
Risky work is sealed off

Up and running in minutes

If your team uses Docker, getting started is one command. Setup then walks you through your AI provider and chat apps. Until you decide otherwise, Bothy is only reachable from the machine it runs on.

  1. Start Bothy

    docker run -d --name bothy -p 127.0.0.1:8080:8080 -v bothy-data:/data ghcr.io/rielicat/bothy:latest

    The same image is on Docker Hub as rielicat/bothy.

  2. Open the setup link

    docker logs bothy

    This shows a one-time setup link. Open it to create the owner account and connect your AI provider and chat apps.
